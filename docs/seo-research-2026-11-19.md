## Why these

- Small independent product sites already rank on page one for these searches (finart.app, merakharcha.in and lekhhaa.com for "personal expense tracker app india"; tldrmoney.in for "without sms permission"; trakio.co.in for "loan tracker app"). A product page can compete; the head terms are held by Play Store listings and list articles.
- "No SMS, no bank login" is the angle the ranking pages lead with, and it is true of Sable.

## Left alone on purpose

- "bank statement converter" and "bank statement to excel" (33,100 and 880): Sable imports a statement into its own records; it does not convert to Excel.
- "bank statement analyzer" (260): the results are tools for lenders.
- "split expenses app" (720): not a Sable feature.
- "hisab kitab app" (3,600): people searching it want a specific app of that name.
